1 · Problem

A fly count alone has three blind spots

  • Every photo is counted as it comes. Glare, blur, a dark or infrared frame, a hand in front of the trap or a camera that no longer sees it all produce a number. One camera in our data filmed a building: every "fly" it reported was false.
  • A count is not a catch. Flies stay in the trap; the signal is what changed since the previous photo.
  • The camera never reacts. Nothing asks for a better photo, changes the exposure or the schedule, or shows a doubtful photo to a person.

Who it is for

Fruit growers and farm managers in Vietnam who watch the oriental fruit fly (Bactrocera spp.) with lure traps and yellow sticky cards, and the agronomists who advise them.

They use FlyBot's web app in Vietnamese or English, its chatbot and Telegram, with EZVIZ cloud cameras they already own or our open ESP32-S3 trap camera.

2 · Solution

Perceive, decide, let a person confirm, act, look again

PerceiveOpenCV 5 on each photo and the previous one: count, quality, trap, change, occlusion, faces
→
Decidean agent under guardrails: a new photo, other image settings, a review, an alert, or nothing
→
Confirma confirm card in FlyBot and on Telegram; nothing reaches a camera without a person
→
ActFlyBot captures or changes the camera; the new photo starts the loop again
  • Bad photos are not counted, and the reason is shown.
  • Every decision leaves a trace: what OpenCV measured, what the agent decided, which tools it called.
  • Growers can ask the chatbot "why did the count jump?": the answer comes from OpenCV's numbers, never from the pixels.

5 · Agent and human approval

A guarded agent that only proposes

  • Rule planner on every photo; Claude Haiku 5.5 for grower questions, with a rule fallback.
  • 14 typed tools; guardrails: run states, budgets, quiet hours, battery, grounded proposals, kill switch.
  • Every action is a proposal; only a person's Confirm runs it; cards expire after 24 h.
  • No photo ever goes to an LLM: it gets OpenCV's numbers as text.

11 · Limits and next steps

What is not done yet

Limitations

  • Little human ground truth: one reviewer's 50 verdicts (6 needless recaptures, 1 unsure; fix re-scored, not re-reviewed), 0 count labels.
  • YOLO26n misses small flies in full lure-trap views; no real sticky-card photo yet.
  • Agent evidence mostly from the earlier build and fake devices.
  • ESP32-S3 field operation (4G, battery, solar) not measured; one region, one server.

Next

  • The advisor reviews the new lure-trap glare limit on fresh photos; label 150 trap photos.
  • Better recall on small flies in lure-trap views.
  • The ESP32-S3 camera in the orchard on 4G and solar, with its battery measured.
  • Yellow sticky cards; OpenCV 5's in-library VLM as a second opinion on doubtful photos.
